Theme Persistence 💾 Saved

Day 1 — Week 7

Toggle the theme below. Your preference is saved to localStorage — refresh the page and it will remember which mode you chose.

☀️ Light 🌙 Dark

How it works

When you toggle, we save "dark" or "light" to localStorage. On page load, we read it back and apply the class before anything renders — so there's no flash of the wrong theme.

Why this matters

User preferences should survive page refreshes. Without localStorage, every visit starts from scratch. With it, the app feels like it actually remembers you.

Try it!

Toggle to dark mode, then refresh the page. It should stay dark. Toggle back to light and refresh again — it stays light. That's localStorage doing its job.
